CAD inspection conventions
  SI units; radii describe gas-path boundaries. Mean = arithmetic span midpoint.
  Legacy P_n/T_n keys denote total pressure/temperature. Static values are labeled explicitly.
  Free vortex: r*C_theta constant, uniform meridional velocity across each span.
  alpha = atan2(C_theta,C_meridional); beta = atan2(C_theta-omega*r,C_meridional).
  Signed blade-flow angles: degrees from local meridional, positive with rotation; meridional is axial except at compressor outlet.
  Compressor outlet uses a constant assumed meridional angle from axial (positive outward), fixed flow-normal area and unchanged meanline speed.
  Compressor outlet radii and axial offsets describe an exit edge normal to that flow, relative to its midpoint (downstream z positive).
  Equivalent shaft-normal section and optimizer reference radii are labeled separately; optimizer geometry, mass and constraints are unchanged.
  Flow angles assume zero incidence/deviation for a first blade sketch.
  Legacy compressor/outlet turbine angles may use positive opposing-rotation magnitudes. Use 'signed' keys for CAD.
  Legacy 'tip' and 'shroud' both denote the outer gas-path boundary.
  NGV exit is before coolant return; rotor inlet is after mixing. Turbine rotor exit swirl is zero.
  Compressor outlet alpha also gives the diffuser inlet flow angle. NGV inlet geometry is not resolved.
  Combustor radii/length/thickness reuse mass-model assumptions; bulk speed/residence use total-state density (low-Mach estimate).
  Chord estimates use span/1.5; blade count, camber, thickness, stagger, row gaps and absolute axial positions remain unspecified.
  Nozzle exit is the cycle's fully expanded circular area; a centrebody requires a new annulus layout.
  Fan inlet coupled hub follows LPT inlet; mass flow uses the outlet annulus. Inlet angles use the model inlet speed.
  Fan outlet swirl is inferred from Euler work with zero inlet swirl; axial speed uses outlet annulus continuity. No downstream deswirler is modeled.
  Fan nozzle annular exit radii assume the rotor outlet hub continues downstream.
  Nonfinite inferred fan angles mean that this added diagnostic has no physical solution.
